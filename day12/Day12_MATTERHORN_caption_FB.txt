Gastric cancer trial: you still give FLOT alone? Adding durvalumab cut relapse or death by 29%.

MATTERHORN (NEJM 2025) was a global, double-blind phase 3 trial in 948 patients with resectable gastric or junction adenocarcinoma: durvalumab or placebo with perioperative FLOT (2 cycles before and 2 after surgery), then 10 cycles of durvalumab or placebo.

Two-year event-free survival: 67.4% vs 58.5% (HR 0.71, 95% CI 0.58-0.86, P < 0.001).
Pathological complete response: 19.2% vs 7.2%.
Interim two-year overall survival: 75.7% vs 70.4%.
Surgery delayed: 10.1% vs 10.8%.

Take-home: durvalumab plus FLOT is the new perioperative standard; final overall survival is awaited.
