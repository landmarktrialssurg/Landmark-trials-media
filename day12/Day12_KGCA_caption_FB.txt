Gastric cancer guideline: you still take the spleen for hilar nodes? Korea's 2024 guideline says no.

Korean Practice Guidelines for Gastric Cancer 2024 (KGCA, J Gastric Cancer 2025; 5th edition, GRADE):

1. Early cancer: endoscopic resection for well or moderately differentiated early cancers; ESD or gastrectomy for expanded indications; surgery if non-curative; eradicate H. pylori to prevent a second cancer.
2. Extent: D1+ for cT1N0; pylorus-preserving gastrectomy if 5 cm or more from the pylorus; proximal gastrectomy with double-tract reconstruction for upper-third early cancer.
3. Advanced cancer: no prophylactic splenectomy for hilar nodes (strong against, high-quality evidence); Siewert II/III: transhiatal rather than transthoracic.
4. Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy for stage I and for locally advanced cancer; robotic gastrectomy an option.
5. Adjuvant S-1 or CAPOX after D2 for stage II/III; no adjuvant chemoradiation; neoadjuvant chemo can be considered.
6. Stage IV: trastuzumab (HER2+), anti-PD-1 with chemo (HER2-), zolbetuximab (claudin 18.2+); no reduction gastrectomy.
