Gastric cancer guideline for Asia: upfront D2 gastrectomy, then adjuvant chemo, is still the standard.

Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guideline for gastric cancer (ESMO Open 2024): ESMO and JSMO with 10 Asian oncology societies, including India; 20 voting experts; every recommendation 100% accepted.

1. Workup: 5-8 biopsies; test HER2, PD-L1, MSI and claudin 18.2; CT thorax, abdomen and pelvis; no routine PET-CT; laparoscopy with washings for perioperative chemo candidates.
2. Surgery: endoscopic resection for mucosal, differentiated, non-ulcerated cancers up to 2 cm; D2 gastrectomy in a high-volume centre; 3 cm margin (5 cm for poorly cohesive).
3. Chemo before surgery: only for selected patients above stage IB, such as cT4 or bulky nodes; FLOT or DOS, or a doublet. Four of the ten societies did not accept FLOT.
4. Chemo after upfront D2 (stage II or higher): S-1 for 1 year, CAPOX for 6 months, or S-1 with docetaxel; doublets preferred for stage III.
5. Metastatic: platinum plus fluoropyrimidine, with trastuzumab, nivolumab (CPS 5 or more) or zolbetuximab by biomarker; no gastrectomy except for palliation.
