We stand on the shoulders of Giants

Jules-Émile Péan (1830-1898) attempted the first gastrectomy for stomach cancer in 1879, two years before Billroth, gave us the Péan clamp, and was among the first to remove an ovarian cyst (1864) and to operate on bladder diverticula (1895). Watch through his story.
