We stand on the shoulders of Giants

Allen Oldfather Whipple (1881-1963) made the pancreaticoduodenectomy reproducible, reporting it in 1935 for carcinoma of the ampulla of Vater. He also gave us Whipple's triad for insulinoma. Swipe through his story.
