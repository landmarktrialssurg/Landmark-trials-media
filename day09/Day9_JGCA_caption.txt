You still open for stage II gastric cancer? Japan says stop.

JGCA 2025 (7th edition), the Japanese gastric cancer treatment guidelines:
ESD for early cancer within criteria.
D2 for cT2+ or node-positive cancer; D1 or D1+ for cT1N0.
No routine splenectomy, no bursectomy.
Laparoscopic distal gastrectomy strongly recommended, even for stage II and beyond; robotic gastrectomy weakly recommended.
Adjuvant chemotherapy after a curative D2: S-1 or CAPOX for pStage II; docetaxel plus S-1, SOX or CAPOX for pStage III.

Pairs with today's trial: the Dutch D1D2 trial.
