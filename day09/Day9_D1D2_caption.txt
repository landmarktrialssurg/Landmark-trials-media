Gastric cancer: D1 or D2 lymphadenectomy?

The Dutch D1D2 trial randomised 1,078 patients in 80 hospitals (1989-1993); 711 had curative surgery (380 D1, 331 D2).

Early on, D2 looked worse: postoperative deaths 10% vs 4%, reoperations 18% vs 8%, much of it from spleen and pancreas resection.
At 15 years: gastric-cancer-related death 37% with D2 vs 48% with D1 (P = 0.01). Overall survival 29% vs 21%, not significant.

Take-home: D2 is the standard, done safely, with spleen and pancreas preserved, in experienced hands.

Save this for your next gastrectomy.

Ref: Songun I, Putter H, Kranenbarg EM, Sasako M, van de Velde CJ. Lancet Oncol. 2010;11(5):439-449. doi:10.1016/S1470-2045(10)70070-X
