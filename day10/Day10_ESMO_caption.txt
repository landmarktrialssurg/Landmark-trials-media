Resectable gastric cancer: chemotherapy alone is no longer the preferred option in the new ESMO guideline.

ESMO's 2026 guideline now covers gastric and oesophageal cancer together and replaces the 2022 gastric guideline. For the stomach:

1. Staging: endoscopy with biopsies and CT thorax, abdomen and pelvis. Diagnostic laparoscopy (with or without washings) for perioperative chemo candidates. PET not routine in gastric cancer.
2. Biomarkers on every tumour: HER2, PD-L1, MSI/dMMR, claudin 18.2.
3. Surgery: ESD for suitable early cancers; D2 gastrectomy in high-volume centres; laparoscopy an option in expert hands.
4. cT2+ or node-positive: perioperative FLOT, with durvalumab plus maintenance preferred where available (MATTERHORN).
5. Junction/oesophageal adenocarcinoma: FLOT over CROSS (ESOPEC); adjuvant nivolumab for PD-L1 positive residual disease.

Pairs with today's trial: MAGIC.
