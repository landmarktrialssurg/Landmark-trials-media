We stand on the shoulders of Giants

Baruch "Barry" Blumberg found the Australia antigen, the surface coat of the hepatitis B virus, and his work led to the blood test and the vaccine that followed. Nobel Prize 1976. Every HBsAg in your preoperative workup goes back to him. Swipe through his story.
